About WiFi Health Check

WiFi Health Check is a focused iPhone app for quick WiFi and LAN troubleshooting, built by OpsHome.

What WiFi Health Check is

WiFi Health Check helps users understand common WiFi issues from their iPhone. It checks public internet connectivity, DNS behavior, current WiFi context such as SSID, BSSID, and security type, local gateway context, LAN device discovery, custom LAN TCP reachability, and WiFi roaming behavior.

Why it exists

WiFi problems are often hard to describe. A simple local report can show whether the issue is external connectivity, a LAN endpoint, gateway configuration, WiFi security context, or roaming between access points.

What the app intentionally does not do

WiFi Health Check is not a packet-capture tool and it does not claim to expose radio metrics that ordinary App Store apps cannot reliably obtain from iOS, such as RSSI, channel, frequency band, channel width, or PHY rate. Instead, it focuses on observable connectivity, DNS, TCP, device discovery, latency, timeout, and roaming behavior.

Official App Store listing

WiFi Health Check is available on the Apple App Store. The App Store currently lists the product as WiFi HealthCheck; it is the same application presented here as WiFi Health Check.
